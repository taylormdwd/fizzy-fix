Frequently Asked

How far in advance should I book?

Most weekends in spring and fall fill 2 to 3 months out. For weddings or large corporate events we recommend booking 4 to 6 months ahead. Last-minute? DM us and we'll see what we can do.

Do you travel outside the East Valley?

Yes. Our usual range covers Gilbert, Mesa, Chandler, Tempe, Scottsdale, Phoenix, Glendale, Peoria, and Queen Creek. Travel outside that range may include a small fee, just ask.

What's included in each package?

Every package includes the trailer or cart, our staff, cups, ice, and the drink lineup you pick. Custom signature drinks, themed cups, and extended hours are available as add-ons.

What if my event is outdoors?

We thrive outdoors. We'll need a flat space to park the trailer plus access to a power source or a generator (we can help arrange one). For Arizona summer events, ask us about shade setup options.

Do you offer sugar-free or non-caffeinated options?

Yes! Most flavors can be made with sugar-free bases, and we always have a no-caffeine option on the menu. Let us know your guest list's needs and we'll build the menu around it.

Will you create a custom drink for my event?

Absolutely — that's our favorite ask. We'll build a signature drink in your colors, your theme, with a custom name. Available as part of every package on request.

What's your cancellation policy?

Deposits are non-refundable but transferable to a new date within 12 months, subject to availability. Full policy is included in your booking confirmation.
